# Code of conduct

Be respectful and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block people who do not follow this. To report a problem, use the private reporting option on the repository's Security tab and mark it as a conduct report.

This is a short statement in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies where this text is silent.
